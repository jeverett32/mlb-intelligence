# License FAQ

This project is released under the **Business Source License 1.1** (BUSL-1.1).

The goal is to keep the source available for learning and internal use, while
preventing third parties from packaging/hosting/selling the application as a
paid product.

## What is allowed?

Generally allowed (see `LICENSE` for the actual terms):

- **Personal use** (run it locally)
- **Internal use** at a company / fund (including for-profit)
- **Modifying** the code for your own internal needs
- Using the model/predictions to **place your own bets**

## What is not allowed?

Not allowed without a separate commercial agreement:

- Selling the software (or a fork) as a **paid product**
- Offering it as a **hosted/managed service (SaaS)** to third parties
- Bundling it into a paid platform where the app itself is a monetized feature

## I want to run this as a paid product/service. What should I do?

Contact the Licensor to discuss a commercial license.

## Is this OSI “open source”?

No. BUSL is **source-available** and includes restrictions.

## Why use BUSL?

It encourages transparency and reuse for internal workflows while protecting the
core application from being repackaged/hosted/sold by third parties.
