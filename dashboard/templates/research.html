Current Pipeline

Model Methodology

Our current model utilizes a Logistic Regression architecture to produce well-calibrated win probabilities across 2,430 games per season.

  • FIP vs ERA Research indicates FIP stabilizes in ~100 innings and is a significantly better predictor of future performance by stripping away defensive variance.
  • wOBA & wRC+ Our offensive features rely on linear-weighted metrics. Unlike Batting Average, these show a 0.95+ correlation with actual run scoring.
  • LR Robustness Logistic Regression provides a robust baseline that avoids over-fitting common in GBDT models while naturally producing calibrated probabilities.
  • Closing Line Value We track CLV as our primary KPI. Beating the closing line in efficient markets is the most reliable indicator of a long-term edge.
Future Roadmap

Strategic Roadmap

We are actively developing the next generation of features to capture situational advantages often missed by market averages.

  • Catcher Framing Integrating Framing Runs Above Average to account for a catcher's ability to convert balls to strikes (swinging win prob by 2-5%).
  • The Bullpen Effect Tracking high-leverage reliever fatigue (pitches in last 48h) and the "Times Through the Order" penalty to refine late-inning probability.
  • Environmental Physics Moving beyond static Park Factors to include coefficients for temperature and air density, affecting ball flight distance.
  • Statcast Matchups Utilizing Exit Velocity and Launch Angle to calculate batter-pitcher mismatch scores against specific pitch types.
